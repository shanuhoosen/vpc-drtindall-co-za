About

Meet Dr Renton Tindall

Dr Renton Tindall qualified as a general dentist and then as a specialist in orthodontics at the University of the Witwatersrand (BDS MDent). He has since been dedicated to orthodontic practice, using the latest technology and regularly attending seminars and courses to stay at the forefront of the field.

At Dr Tindall's practice, each patient receives his undivided attention in all aspects of orthodontic care. A great smile isn't just about appearance — it promotes oral comfort, stability, and may prevent joint discomfort, facial pain, and headaches.
